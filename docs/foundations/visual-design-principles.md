## Use Neutral UI Color Scheme

Base Colors: Use a clean, neutral palette for nebulaONE UI, such as shades of gray, soft whites, and subtle reds or greens. These colors work well with most brand palettes and won’t clash with client logos or colors.

Accent Colors: Use a limited number of subtle accent colors for highlights, borders, or call-to-action buttons. Soft, muted colors can provide contrast while allowing the client’s branding (like their logo) to stand out.

## Hierarchy and Clarity

Prioritize important information and actions, using visual hierarchy to guide users. Larger text, bold icons, and prominent colors should emphasize primary actions and critical information.

Group related elements together and create a clear flow to reduce confusion, helping users move through tasks smoothly.


## Effective Use of Visuals and Icons

Use icons and visuals to simplify complex ideas (e.g., settings, bot modules, analytics), helping users understand features at a glance.

Ensure that icons are universally understood, with accompanying labels for clarity.

## Guided Branding Customization

**Let clients express their identity without compromising the integrity of the product experience.**

N1 should feel like a natural extension of each client's digital ecosystem, while maintaining a consistent, accessible, and intuitive user experience.

Client branding should enhance familiarity, trust, and institutional identity, not introduce unnecessary complexity or compromise usability.

### Design guidelines

1. **Customise identity, not interaction.** Allow clients to personalise logos, colours, typography, imagery, and other approved brand elements. Keep navigation, interaction patterns, and core behaviours consistent.

2. **Define clear boundaries.** Use a controlled theming system with approved design tokens and components. Avoid unrestricted CSS injection or customisations that undermine product quality.

3. **Keep branding contextual.** Apply institutional branding at the organisation level and agent-specific branding where it helps users recognise and distinguish official agents. Avoid overwhelming the interface with competing visual identities.

4. **Make customisation scalable.** Branding should be easy to configure, preview, maintain, and update without requiring custom development for each client.

5. **Protect the product's visual integrity.** Maintain a coherent design language across all client environments. Customisation should never make N1 feel like a different product with unfamiliar behaviours.
